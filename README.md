# SiteCopilot AI

## Overview

SiteCopilot AI is an AI-powered field operations platform designed to simplify construction site management in Kenya and across Africa. The platform helps contractors, site engineers, project managers, and field teams automate daily operations, improve communication, and gain real-time visibility into project progress.

Rather than replacing existing workflows, SiteCopilot AI integrates with how construction teams already work—using photos, voice notes, WhatsApp, and mobile devices—to reduce paperwork, prevent costly mistakes, and improve decision-making.

## Problem

Construction projects often rely on paper records, spreadsheets, phone calls, and WhatsApp for managing site activities. This leads to delayed reporting, poor coordination, material losses, communication gaps, and limited visibility into project progress.

SiteCopilot AI aims to become the intelligent assistant that coordinates field operations, allowing teams to spend less time on administration and more time building.

## Vision

To become a leading AI-powered operating system for field operations, starting with construction and expanding into infrastructure, utilities, mining, telecommunications, and other industries with distributed field teams.

## Status

🚧 MVP in development.

The first release will focus on solving the most time-consuming daily tasks for construction teams while laying the foundation for a scalable AI-powered platform.
